Upload CBT file

Upload a .json, .csv, .pdf, or .docx past-question paper. You must pick the subject and year — students will practise it as a timed CBT on the website and desktop. PDF/Word files with selectable text are extracted into questions (scanned photo PDFs are not supported).

Review extracted questions
